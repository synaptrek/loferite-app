Support

Found a problem, or have an idea for Loferite? Write to loferite [at] tuta [dot] io.

The quickest way is from inside the app: Settings ▸ Support ▸ Send feedback. This opens a pre-filled email in your Mail app with the app version, device model and iOS version, so you don't have to look them up. You review the message and send it yourself.

Please don't include personal, patient or research-subject information, or any images, in your message.

Privacy policy

In short: Loferite does not collect any data. Everything you import stays on your device.

On-device only
Images, volumes, meshes, tractography and EEG recordings you import are copied into the app's private storage and never leave the device.
No network, no tracking
The app makes no network connections with your data, and includes no analytics, tracking or advertising.
Not synced or backed up
Your files and the library index are excluded from backups (including cloud backups) and are not accessible through the Files app.
Diagnostics log
A local activity log (Settings ▸ Diagnostics) stays on this device, is size-capped and trimmed automatically, and can be cleared at any time. It is never sent automatically; a redacted copy is included with feedback only if you choose to attach it.
Feedback (optional)
If you choose to send feedback (Settings ▸ Support ▸ Send feedback), your device's Mail app opens a message that you review and send yourself. Nothing is sent automatically. It contains only a short technical diagnostic (app version, device model, iOS version) and, strictly if you opt in, a redacted copy of the activity log. It never includes images, volumes, patient data or file names.
When you email the developer
If you send an email, the developer receives your email address and the content of your message, and uses them only to reply to you and to fix the problem you report. This is done on the basis of the developer's legitimate interest in supporting the app (and, where a contract applies, to perform it). Messages are kept for as long as needed to handle your request, and no longer than 12 months, unless the law requires otherwise. They are not shared with anyone or used for any other purpose. You can ask for them to be deleted at any time by writing to loferite [at] tuta [dot] io.
Data you import
The developer has no access to anything you import into Loferite. For that data you, or your institution, remain the data controller, and you are responsible for having the right to use it.
Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data the developer holds about you (in practice, only your emails), to object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority. In the EU/EEA and UK these rights come from the GDPR; in Italy the authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. To exercise any right, write to loferite [at] tuta [dot] io.
Children
Loferite is intended for adult researchers and students. It is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.
This website
This page uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; as with any website, the hosting provider may record technical data such as IP addresses in its server logs, under GitHub's privacy statement.
Deleting data
Because nothing leaves the device, removing a study, or deleting the app, permanently removes the associated data.
Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
Contact
Loferite is developed by the individual listed as the seller on the App Store. For any privacy question, write to loferite [at] tuta [dot] io.
